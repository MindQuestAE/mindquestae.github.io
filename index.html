02 Round two

How the game is played.

Two stages, one champion team. Here’s what to expect on the day.

  1. 1

    Written prelims

    Every team sits a written round of category-based questions. The top scorers advance.

  2. 2

    Grand Finale

    Finalists go head-to-head on stage in interactive buzzer rounds — with a live audience cheering.

  3. 3

    Awards

    The MindQuest 2026 champions are crowned, with prizes for the podium and special awards.

Who can enter

Open to all high school students of the Indian diaspora.

  • Grade 8–12 of the Indian curriculum
  • Year 9–13 of the British curriculum or equivalent
  • Currently enrolled in a UAE school
  • Valid student ID required

House rules

Keep it fair, keep it fun.

  • Teams of two participants
  • No external aids or devices
  • Punctuality is mandatory
  • Respect all participants
  • The Quiz Master’s decision is final

Breaking the rules may lead to disqualification. Questions? Contact us.

Frequently asked.

Can’t find your answer? Ask us directly.

Who can take part?

High school students of the Indian diaspora — Grade 8–12 of the Indian curriculum, or Year 9–13 of the British curriculum or equivalent — currently enrolled in a UAE school. Valid school ID is required.

Is there a registration fee?

No. Registration for MindQuest is completely free.

What topics are covered?

General knowledge including, but not limited to, the World, India, Science & Technology, Current Affairs, Sports, Literature, and Arts & Entertainment.

Are there prizes for winners?

Yes. Winners receive cash prizes, trophies, medals and certificates. All Grand Finale qualifiers receive certificates of excellence.

Can parents attend?

Yes. Parents and family members are welcome to attend the Grand Finale and cheer on the participants.
