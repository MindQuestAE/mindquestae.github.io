Principles

Four ideas that every screen should follow.

It’s a game board

Sections are “Rounds”, cards look like question cards, and the hero lets visitors actually play.

Ink on paper

Warm paper background, 2px Oxford Blue outlines and solid offset shadows. No gradients, no blur, no soft shadows.

Yellow is the highlighter

Minion Yellow marks the one thing that matters most in a view: the main action, the answer, the winner.

Loud headings, calm copy

Heavy Montserrat headlines ending in a full stop; short, friendly Lato sentences underneath.

Colour

The four brand colours are unchanged from the MindQuest brand guidelines. Tints and the paper surface are derived from them. Use the CSS custom properties, never raw hex values.

Typography

Montserrat (--font-title) for headings, labels and buttons; Lato (--font-body) for body text. Headings use tight negative letter-spacing; labels are uppercase with wide tracking. Both load with font-display: swap behind metric-matched Arial fallbacks (“Montserrat Fallback”, “Lato Fallback”), so text never shifts when the web fonts arrive.

Shape & depth

✓Do

  • End headlines with a full stop.
  • Write sentence case everywhere except uppercase labels.
  • Mark unknown facts “To be announced” rather than guessing.
  • Label anything from last year as Season 1 or 2025.

×Don’t

  • Use gradients, glass blur or soft drop shadows.
  • Put more than one yellow primary button in a view.
  • Set body text in yellow or sky blue on light surfaces.
  • Introduce colours outside the brand palette (error red excepted).

Accessibility

AreaRule
Text contrastInk on paper, white, yellow and sky; white or sky on ink. Never yellow or sky text on a light surface.
FocusAll interactive elements show a 3px yellow outline with an ink ring (:focus-visible). Try tabbing through this page.
IconsIcons are inline SVGs from the sprite at the top of <body>: <svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-name">. They inherit currentColor and size with font-size. Decorative icons carry aria-hidden="true"; icon-only links need an aria-label.
Touch targetsButtons and menu items are at least 44px tall on mobile.
SemanticsOne h1; each section has an h2; accordions use details/summary; a skip link precedes the navbar.
MotionRespect prefers-reduced-motion (see Motion).
